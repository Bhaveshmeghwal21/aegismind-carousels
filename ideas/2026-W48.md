topic: Psychology
hook: You test your own ideas the wrong way
caption: In 1960 Peter Wason gave people the numbers 2, 4, 6 and asked them to find the secret rule. They only tried guesses that fit their theory, like 8, 10, 12, and many announced the wrong rule with full confidence. The real rule was any ascending numbers. That is confirmation bias: we look for proof, not for what could break our idea. Before your next strong opinion, ask what would prove you wrong. #psychology #confirmationbias #cognitivebias #thinking

topic: Psychology
hook: Nobody is watching you as closely as you think
caption: In 2000, psychologists at Cornell had students wear an embarrassing Barry Manilow T-shirt into a room of people. The students guessed that about half the room noticed it. About a quarter had. It is called the spotlight effect: we feel centre stage in our own lives, so we overestimate how much others notice us. Remember this before your next awkward moment. #psychology #spotlighteffect #socialanxiety #mindset

caption: Epictetus was born a slave around 50 CE, was later freed, and became one of the most influential Stoic teachers. He wrote nothing himself; his student Arrian recorded his lectures.
