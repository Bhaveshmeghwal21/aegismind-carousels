topic: Psychology
hook: You test your own ideas the wrong way
slides:
- The 2-4-6 puzzle :: In 1960 Peter Wason told people the numbers 2, 4, 6 follow a secret rule. Their job was to find the rule.
- What people did :: They tried 8, 10, 12 and 20, 22, 24. Every guess fit their theory. Almost nobody tried to break it.
- The real rule :: Any ascending numbers. Because people only looked for proof, many announced the wrong rule with full confidence.
- Confirmation bias :: We look for evidence that agrees with us. Disagreement takes effort, so we skip it.
- Your move :: Before your next strong opinion, ask: what would prove me wrong? Then go and look for it.
caption: We rarely test what we believe. We just collect proof. What would change your mind? #psychology #confirmationbias #cognitivebias #thinking

hook: Camus said we must imagine Sisyphus happy
slides:
- The punishment :: In the Greek myth, Sisyphus pushes a boulder up a hill. It rolls back down. Forever.
- Camus's question :: In The Myth of Sisyphus (1942), Camus asked whether life is worth living if it has no given meaning.
- The twist :: “One must imagine Sisyphus happy.” The struggle itself, he wrote, can fill a human heart.
- The absurd :: The gap between our hunger for meaning and a silent universe. Camus said to live inside it, not run from it.
- Your move :: What is your boulder? Name it. Then decide how you will carry it today.
caption: Meaning may not be given. It may be made in the climb. #camus #absurdism #existentialism #philosophy

topic: Psychology
hook: Nobody is watching you as closely as you think
slides:
- The experiment :: In 2000, psychologists at Cornell had students wear an embarrassing Barry Manilow T-shirt into a room of people.
- The guess :: The students guessed that about half the room had noticed the shirt.
- The truth :: About a quarter had.
- The spotlight effect :: We feel centre stage in our own lives, so we overestimate how much others notice us.
- Your move :: Remember this before your next awkward moment. Most people are busy worrying about themselves.
caption: The spotlight is mostly in your head. #psychology #spotlighteffect #socialanxiety #mindset

topic: Nietzsche
hook: Nietzsche's formula for greatness
slides:
- Amor fati :: Latin for “love of fate.” In Ecce Homo, Nietzsche called it his formula for greatness in a human being.
- What he meant :: Not just to endure what happens. To want nothing to be different, not forward, not backward, not in all eternity.
- Harder than it sounds :: It includes the failures, the losses and the years you wish you could undo.
- The test :: He also asked: could you live your life again, exactly the same, forever?
- Your move :: Pick one thing you regret. Ask what it made possible.
caption: Do not just survive your life. Choose it. #nietzsche #amorfati #philosophy #stoicism

slides:
- Epictetus :: Born a slave around 50 CE. Later freed, he became one of the most influential Stoic teachers.
- What he wrote :: Nothing himself. His student Arrian wrote down his lectures.

- The famous line :: “Men are disturbed not by things, but by the views which they take of them.” Enchiridion 5.
- Your move :: Take one problem today. Split it into what is yours to control and what is not.
